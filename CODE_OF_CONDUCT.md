# Code of conduct

odi is a project for everyone. We want taking part to be welcoming,
respectful and free of harassment, whoever you are.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and take feedback constructively, and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
  background or ability.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, or intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when representing the project elsewhere. Report problems privately to
the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/manoj1604/odi/security/advisories/new)
form (choose it even for conduct reports) or by contacting a maintainer
directly. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content,
and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks these rules.

This code is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
